- Groups accept `exit`: TCP connections through the group dial the selected node first and leave from the exit node; nested groups with an `exit` add theirs from the innermost group out, up to four hops in all. An exit is never probed for being an exit: a probed exit (one that is also a member or `final` of some group) that dies takes every route of the group out of selection, its `final` included unless that is `block`, while an exit used only as an exit stays selectable: ten failed dials counted against it mark it dead and log a WARN, but its group keeps routing through it. A failed dial counts against the hop that failed; a successful one marks every hop available and records latency on the outermost exit. Groups with an `exit` offer no UDP candidates yet, and their UDP connectivity in the datapath says so.
