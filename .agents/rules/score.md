## Score group policy

Read with: `AGENTS.md` (Current validation guidance: the focused group-policy and Score tests); `configuration.md` for group policy settings; `outbound.md` for connection establishment and shared UDP sources. Score is always compiled and has no configuration knobs; omitted policy selects Selector.

### Module ownership

Score `budget` owns currency/in-flight accounting, `evaluation` owns the shared bounded serving pool, `comparison/store` owns bounded observations, `comparison` owns timed pair evidence, `verification` owns the single optional-work dispatch walk and readonly reporting, and `evidence`/`ranking`/`feedback` own outcomes and ordinary decisions; `ranking/dispatch` owns stateful selection entry points, reservations and reporting. Named node provenance and borrowed cell stamps keep parent-incarnation/effective-fence rules shared without merging failure time with reload invalidation. Comparison and validation regression topics use nested test modules.

### Business evidence and attempts

Score keeps business reliability separate from event-timed performance and configured-probe cohorts; never add overlapping global/family/exact completions. Live accepted progress must not multiply terminal outcomes or re-add final byte totals. Neutral cells stay in bounded LRUs to preserve in-flight incarnations and trial recency; admitted business reporters survive reload, but probe baselines are invalidated and old probe publication is fenced. TCP establishment orchestration stays in `control/connection/tcp.rs`, dial/admission in `tcp/dial.rs`; one Score replacement shares the original deadline and recorded final-edge provenance, never display-chain authorization or application replay. Score tests belong under `group/score/tests/`.

URLTest's fresh retry deadline preserves any admitted original Score identity; an unbegun primary has no continuation, while actual admission refusals remain terminal.

Every pool-bound pending Score attempt, including ordinary retries, must still belong to each captured group/network pool at begin. Missing or departed membership cancels pending work and refunds reservations before any stale/unscored path. Unbound stale observation factories may proceed unscored only without a reserved token; preserve their identity and publish no current currency/evidence. Never downgrade invalid optional work into a free challenger dial. Continuations cannot earn in a new group/network/family; `sources.recovery` counts all continuations, including DNS fallback, UI redirects and subscription redirects. Reload refunds pending work but retains started in-flight suppression until settlement/TTL. DNS lifecycle admission is synchronous; only physical-dial admission may wait before I/O.

### Fault attribution

QUIC node provenance belongs to actual proxy-carrier owners, never every `quinn::ConnectionError`; packet-backed DNS endpoints preserve their adapter's terminal cause separately from end-to-end DNS errors. Project it only onto typed endpoint loss, not buffered reset/EOF, remote close or malformed H3 responses; address retry aggregation must retain the cause. H2 stream/user errors and active-session AnyTLS TCP SYNACK timeouts are target/flow-scoped; silent-session/UoT service faults remain node-scoped. VMess zero-byte response-header EOF stays ambiguous I/O, while partial/malformed headers retain carrier provenance. XUDP END|ERROR closes a shared multi-target source and has no single-target attribution.

### Verification and relations

Score verification reports scoped empirical observations, not a statistical error guarantee or optimality certificate. The same evaluator identifies availability/response gaps, schedules bounded real-flow validation and publishes readonly pairwise response relations; missing transfer creates no work and waits for actual offered load. Keep practical equivalence, provisional forwarding and observed usability separate; there is no group-wide comparison claim. Relations expire with their response metric; API reads cannot spend validation budgets or mutate counters.

Score trial suppression requires an actual ordinary escape to a different leaf, not merely a failure/ineligibility reason label. Configured health feedback carries the real restart-required probe interval into immutable cohort identity; keep four-report qualification, cadence-aware checked retention and weaker-side freshness without widening business windows or adding probes. Common response targets qualify before the canonical cap, independent of measured values; directional metrics use that same cohort. Partial matched-target coverage may still inform promotion and relations but must keep the response gap pending.

Readonly relations are independent of completion-dependent switching margins and the action heuristic's millisecond floor: they read the selection's original promotion pairs and classify only a fresh qualified response with the inclusive symmetric 10% band. Reliability, directional and holding protections belong to ordinary selection, not relations.

### Serving pool

Score owns one bounded serving pool per group/network, shared by all parent routes and targets. Demand-sized k = 1 + ⌊D/128⌋ within 3–25 is additionally capped by ⌈√n⌉ + 1 (minimum 3); D is original business projected to the evidence half-life, q = 16. All ordinary, first-choice, escape, recovery and optional picks stay in-pool; no k + 1 winner override. Bootstrap reserves the ordinary first-choice winner within k, and reranking prioritizes ordinary eligibility before utility/probe ties so unqualified peers cannot displace the only usable service. Three node-wide failures or authoritative health exclusion can replace a member inside the same capacity. Preserve absent direct members; immediate subgroup names (not configurable UUIDs) identify representative slots and replacements cannot accumulate stale leaves. Selector Apply must reach its chosen child even when Peek has no visible member. Captured ordinary-pool obligations are checked again at begin, including after reload; only that owner's explicit final is exempt. Already-begun work continues, while a new retry must revalidate membership. Readonly uses committed members or a temporary cold projection and cannot mutate them. A rotation member selected for ordinary service trades places with a ranked member without changing membership; its replacement gets a fresh rotation interval. TCP/UDP remain separate, and active connections can retain retired members. Keep borrowed group-key lookups on the admission path, original budgets, normal rotation and no-substitute recovery.

Full child resolution may withdraw its retained via slot when the chosen path has no eligible candidate; retry-only omissions cannot. IPv4-before-final preflight derives temporary pool membership through the same path under current authority, without committing pools, counters or work. Public Peek remains committed-only after initialization.

### Optional work and trials

Optional work has one dispatch walk, shared with the readonly question/action/wait: evaluated challengers with an open question (recovery, availability, a pair when ordinary selection could compare them but the selection's original pair lacks a fresh non-partial business response of the scope newer than its degradation, qualification), untrained first, then most progress toward that question, then least recently selected. Each member has at most one unanswered trial per open question and target (qualification counts across targets); except for recovery, a member is dispatched only while its completions plus unfinished work stay below the selection's completions, so cold exploration starts after the selection's first completion. Configured probes never settle a pair question; there is no joint alignment across challengers. A budget refusal ends the walk, an in-flight refusal tries the next member, and recency advances only when attributed work is admitted at begin.

Trial settlement preserves historical cost/outcome counters after reload, but only currently configured `(group, node)` members contribute policy yield. Removed members cannot pause or accelerate replacement funding; retained members may contribute across authority changes.

Out-of-pool members receive no new ordinary or optional selections. Previously reserved policy work retains its token, identity and TTL checks but must also revalidate current serving membership before begin. Probe baselines remain available, while comparison-cell admission normally follows the evaluation set, with exceptions for up to four recent Apply winners and initial/no-initialized-set collection. Recent failure exclusion removes an ineligible member from pending validation; exclusion is not measured equivalence. Response degradation compares only the same exact target; aggregate comparisons reopen on carrier pressure, not mixed-target spikes.

### Availability evidence

Observed availability uses four distinct targeted Traffic reporters with eligible RX after setup/TX per uninterrupted cell cohort, not terminal completions. Cell epochs and per-reporter/per-cell credits fence failure, reload, silence of at least 60 seconds and incarnation replacement; clones, repeated RX and settlement cannot multiply credits. Neutral settlement may flush eligible RX only at its actual event time. First post-failure RX clears exploration delay for funded half-open work; four own-cell post-failure reporters clear its hard episode and grant current recovery qualification without resetting numeric history. Availability alone never grants cold/reload-only qualification, and another target's aggregate recovery cannot supply exact credits.
Shared-source replies may precede sender-task TX bookkeeping. Only the first successfully accepted positive send may reconcile that reporter's already delivered RX at or after its serialized send start and setup completion; capture start after source-gate waits, preserve actual RX time and all cell fences, and never turn queued/failed/retired sends or ordinary TX-only callbacks into receive evidence.
TCP copy/splice relay errors retain socket-side provenance and their original I/O cause. Only client-side disconnects (`ConnectionReset`, `ConnectionAborted`, `BrokenPipe`, `NotConnected`, `UnexpectedEof`, `TimedOut`, `HostUnreachable`, `NetworkUnreachable`) settle Score as cancellation: preserve factual RX and prior failures, never grant success or clear backoff. The same upstream errors, protocol errors, and unclassified local failures keep their existing failure treatment; connection-error telemetry remains unchanged. Score neutrality comes from this disconnect classification, not from log severity or error kind alone.

### Ordinary selection and currency

Score ordinary incumbent protection is separate from verification. Keep first-choice utility and immediate escape from ineligibility/unresolved failure inside the shared serving pool. Target failures retain numeric outcomes but mutate only exact hard state; typed node/carrier faults and unknown pre-setup/targetless failures remain node-conservative. Dependent cells inherit node incarnation, failure time and shared-source episode highwater; late fanout counts flows without refencing a recovered episode. Probe failures invalidate only their current slot, unrelated target failures never invalidate probes. Preserve typed causes through shared writer closure, child fanout, cancellation cleanup and QUIC-stall classification. RX recovery respects failure/reload and started-cell fences; publication is event-driven and capped at once per second per reporter. Ordinary cold qualification still needs four effective completions, and historical reliability/hold maturity use actual decayed counts without overlapping sums. Healthy promotion compares every eligible challenger in the evaluation set against the fixed incumbent, using eight canonical equal-weight common targets and 15-second blocks expiring at start+60s; pair results are indexed by node, never found by a member-by-store scan. Currency obeys `spent + reserved <= B + E`, where each original start adds 1/q to E with q = 16, or q = 8 while the group/network's decayed original business (five-minute half-life) implies at least 2 flows/s and the scope has ≥3 recent trial outcomes (ten-minute half-life, decayed weight > 2.5) at ≥80% success; target refusals are not yield outcomes and reload forgets yield. Behind an observed-usable selection, scopes under 20% recent success pause optional trials (≥2 recent failures wait for decay, one allows a single trial in flight; a ledger refusal like exhausted credit: each refused candidate counts as `budgetBlocked` and reads as a `budget` wait, an in-flight member reads `inFlight`; exploration continues without a usable selection, recovery unaffected). B is frozen and earned credit caps at eight; node-work spends before DNS/admission, begun cancellation never refunds, retries/nested recounting never mint credit. Keep readonly relations distinct from promotion, and logical memory distinct from RSS. `ordinarySwitch` excludes first choice/trials/Peek and is distinct from `switchFlap`. No per-packet logs or exported target identities.

### Carrier pressure

Score carrier pressure is advisory node-to-proxy evidence, never business outcome, target performance or ordinary UDP loss. Runtime-owner weak binding and active-authority/publication-time fences own admission. Physical TCP uses length-checked borrowed `TCP_INFO` with event-driven one-second gating; QUIC reuses its existing monitor after confirmation/publication and resets peer/counter baselines. Shared carriers publish sustained episodes, not one event per logical flow. Health-filter family is not socket identity; hints reopen node-scoped questions only for carrier-backed networks, excluding native Shadowsocks UDP and unobserved raw SOCKS/direct paths. Preserve validation budgets, backoff, utility, qualification, pool liveness, Vision Direct and terminal/health semantics. `carrierPressure` and `carrierValidation` are readonly diagnostic counters, not loss rates.
`carrierRttPressure` and `carrierLossPressure` count the reason at fresh-hint admission; a combined episode increments both but only one `carrierPressure`. Duplicate/stale hints add no cause counts. These are overlapping diagnostics, not transport identities, packet loss rates or additional business failures.
